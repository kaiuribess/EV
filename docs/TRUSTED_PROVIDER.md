# Provider-trusted sportsbook research

`reference_quality_mode=trusted_provider` is an explicit research choice for the existing weighted reference profiles. It prices the latest complete, matching ParlayAPI sportsbook pairs without claiming each selection was re-observed within five seconds. `strict_verified` remains the default and retains its five-second exact-source rules.

The two clock limits in trusted mode are **30 seconds by default**: `trusted_max_delivery_age_ms` bounds how long the local calculation may reuse a delivered pair, and `trusted_max_indicator_age_ms` bounds a reported bookmaker/fixture poll or valid quote observation when present. Neither turns a receipt, price-change clock, or polling heartbeat into a selection observation. An unknown quote observation remains unknown. Explicit suspension, withdrawal, cached/degraded evidence, future clocks, and expired available indicators reject the pair. Unknown active status can contribute to labeled research; production use requires the separate, narrowly approved pilot policy and all other execution gates. The current port-8002 research settings use a **five-minute delivered-row window for game moneylines, spreads and totals**. Player-prop research uses a two-minute delivered-row window and a three-minute cap on any available observation or poll indicator. These longer windows are display assumptions for pregame research, not measured quote freshness or permission to trade. The strict five-second rule remains unchanged.

For the documented complete Parlay game-board stream, a one-sided `odds_update` can be combined with the unchanged opposing side from the current baseline for **research only**. The pair's delivery age is measured from the older side, and the dashboard calls out that the sides came from separate frames. Another update to the same side does not renew the opposing side's age. A reconnect replaces the baseline; explicit suspension or withdrawal removes the research pair. Strict verification and native execution never treat this merge as a new observation.

Parlay's broad prop stream and paginated or truncated REST views can contribute a self-contained, exact two-sided row to **research** even when the overall board is not complete. The dashboard labels the incomplete coverage. Partially failed, degraded, cached, or topped-up responses remain ineligible, including when they are also marked truncated. A complete price pair from a bounded board does not establish that other players, lines, books, or removals were delivered.

Changing a pricing setting refreshes the existing source-only reference display without evaluating a native order strategy or arming execution. The dashboard still removes a reference when its bounded clock expires.

The research projector uses Parlay's source event ID, exact teams, scheduled start, period, player, statistic, line and both opposing prices. It keeps source-only markets under `research:parlay:*`, separate from reviewed canonical/native identities. For supported pregame profiles it rejects three-way moneylines, quarter/half rows presented as full game, integer push-capable lines, one-sided scoring props, and conflicting or malformed pairs. Opposite signed spread orientations receive different outcome identities. Books are deduplicated across REST and WebSocket transports before weighting. Missing books are renormalized after the selected profile's distinct-book requirements pass; sharp profiles retain their anchor rules, while the separate multi-book research profile has none.

Currently enabled research profiles cover NBA core props, NFL volume props, MLB pitcher strikeouts, hitter volume and two-sided home runs, and NHL common props, plus NBA/NFL, MLB and NHL main markets. Coverage is conditional on Parlay actually returning complete compatible prices. Other leagues and prop types require an exact profile and source mapping; the shared pricing engine does not invent missing feeds or opposing odds. The winner-only estimate excludes exceptional settlement states. It is not an estimate of realized unconditional profit.

`trusted_pilot_policy` is disabled by default. The production configuration remains research-only. A pilot may name one exact pregame market or a reviewed normal-question rule for Novig NFL receptions/receiving-yards props. The dynamic rule requires at least three independent approved books. It must bind the provider, books, unknown-status/observation-age choices, indicator age, uncertainty allowance, and debit/loss ceilings no greater than $5. The owner's source-quality approval alone does not verify a market's normal condition, approve a native mapping, or arm trading. If the complete policy and all independent launch checks pass, the pricing engine uses the existing native strategy, subtracts the uncertainty allowance from primary and sensitivity probabilities, and enforces quarter-Kelly and both dollar ceilings. Unknown quote-observation age remains `null`; delivery age is labeled separately. The separate strict five-second mode is unchanged.

The one-shot path still requires a current reviewed native mapping, synchronized executable depth, verified fees/minimums, scoped cash, a matching one-attempt launch profile, current account and order reconciliation, risk and location evidence. Any prior durable attempt consumes the one-shot authorization. The kill switch vetoes submission. The owner approved a narrow source-quality assumption for this task, but the running policy remains disabled: no reviewed NFL normal-condition rule, executable Novig mapping, or current account-bound location attestation is loaded. The saved October 22 NBA launch profile is stale and remains disarmed. Synthetic tests do not validate current source prices, placement, fills, or settlement.

The dashboard's **Reference prices** view displays fair probabilities and effective weights even without a native contract. A fair probability is not a positive-EV signal. If an independently reviewed native book maps to that exact canonical market, the same view can also show a read-only, conservative-fee minimum-lot EV comparison. That comparison creates no order intent and checks neither available cash nor quarter-Kelly, risk, and owner authorization. Native matching, executable depth, verified fees, account cash, risk and owner authorization are separate stages. Values disappear when their bounded delivery evidence expires. Historical captures and test fixtures never become current prices.

The command performs no native venue write, configuration change or order placement. It consumes the provider's documented request credits. Do not run it repeatedly just to refresh a screenshot.

Parlay's [speed page](https://parlay-api.com/speed) describes `verified_at`/`is_current` at bookmaker polling scope. Its [detailed documentation](https://parlay-api.com/docs) also warns that fixture/bookmaker checks and retained board rows need not establish that every displayed selection was just observed. A [WebSocket](https://parlay-api.com/docs/websocket) push interval describes delivery/coalescing, not upstream quote age. The existing Business feed's game-line and prop responses must be interpreted under those scopes. For native pricing and settlement, use the applicable [Kalshi API](https://docs.kalshi.com/llms.txt) or [Novig API](https://docs.novig.com/llms.txt) contract rather than sportsbook prices as executable offers.

The Parlay game-board WebSocket uses explicit `bookmakers`, `kinds=game`, and `markets=h2h,spreads,totals` filters. Do **not** add `snapshot=complete` to that subscription: the current documentation reserves that parameter for a restricted native NFL prop scope, and our Business game subscription was rejected with close code 4005 when it was present. A successful game baseline must still carry `snapshot_scope=current_game_board`, `snapshot_complete=true`, and `truncated=false` before it can replace local board state.
